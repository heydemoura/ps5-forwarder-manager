Forwarder Manager now works on its own: nothing else has to be loaded for it to reach your forwarders.

### Changed
- The app bundles the PS5-Lapy-JB-Daemon helper. When no Lapy daemon is running, it starts this helper through the ELF loader, which lifts only Forwarder Manager out of its sandbox and then exits.
- A Lapy daemon that is already running is still used first, as before.
- The message shown when the app cannot reach /data now explains what to check.

### Thanks
- ArkSama and Team PHU, who created PS5-Lapy-JB-Daemon, and mpereiraesaa for the one-shot helper the app bundles.

### Requirements
- A jailbroken PS5 with elfldr on port 9021.
- ShadowMountPlus, to register the app and the forwarders it creates.

### Install
Unzip `PPSA99930.zip` and copy the `PPSA99930` folder to `/data/homebrew/`, replacing the old one. Your forwarders are kept.
